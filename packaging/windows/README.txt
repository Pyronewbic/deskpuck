Deskpuck for Windows
====================

Use a Nintendo Switch 2 Joy-Con as a mouse and keyboard.

Start deskpuck.exe. Its icon appears in the system tray (it may be under
the ^ arrow). Right-click it for the menu; choose Pair New Joy-Con..., then
hold SYNC on the Joy-Con. Do not pair the Joy-Con in Windows Settings.
Left-click the icon for Settings.

Windows may warn that it protected your PC, as Deskpuck is not signed:
choose More info, then Run anyway.

deskpuck-cli.exe is the command-line tool: deskpuck-cli --help.

More: https://github.com/Pyronewbic/deskpuck
